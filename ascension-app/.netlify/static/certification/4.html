Fraud, Waste & Abuse

FWA definitions, reporting obligations, and penalties.

Create with ElevenLabs: character welcome, key concept explainer, or scene transition

Learning Objectives

  • •Define fraud, waste, and abuse in the Medicare context
  • •Identify major FWA laws and regulations
  • •Recognize consequences and penalties
  • •Know how to report suspected FWA
  • •Understand corrective action requirements

Key Content

  • Fraud: Knowingly submitting false claims or misrepresentations to get payment. Requires intent.
  • Waste: Practices that result in unnecessary costs (e.g., overuse). Generally not criminally negligent.
  • Abuse: Practices that don't meet standards of care or provide medically necessary services.
  • 18 USC 1347: Criminal health care fraud — up to 10 years imprisonment, fines up to $250,000.
  • FWA training: Within 90 days of hire, annually thereafter. Required for all Sponsor and FDR employees.
  • Report suspected FWA through your organization's compliance process. Confidential, good-faith reporting is protected.

Key takeaway

Detect, correct, prevent. Reporting is everyone's responsibility.

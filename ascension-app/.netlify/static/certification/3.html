Compliance & Marketing

CMS marketing rules, SOA, educational vs marketing events, compensation.

Create with ElevenLabs: character welcome, key concept explainer, or scene transition

Learning Objectives

  • •Identify CMS marketing requirements for MA and Part D
  • •Explain Scope of Appointment (SOA) and timing rules
  • •Distinguish educational events from marketing events
  • •Understand agent compensation and pro-rata recoupment
  • •Recognize prohibited marketing practices

Key Content

  • CMS requires accurate, non-misleading marketing. No pressure, no false deadlines, no claims that plans cover 'everything.'
  • Scope of Appointment (SOA) is required before discussing specific plans. Valid 12 months from signature.
  • Educational events vs marketing: 12+ hours between them. Educational = no plan-specific discussion.
  • At educational events: light snacks ($15 or less per person), business reply cards. No full meals, no gift cards.
  • Agent compensation: Pro-rata recoupment when members disenroll. Varies by plan and timing.
  • Employer/union marketing: Employer must notify retirees before you contact them. Materials may be reviewed by CMS on request.

Key takeaway

When in doubt, follow CMS scripts and document everything.

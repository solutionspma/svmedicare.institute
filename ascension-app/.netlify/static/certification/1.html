Medicare Program Basics

Eligibility, Parts A & B, Original Medicare, premiums, and Medigap.

Create with ElevenLabs: character welcome, key concept explainer, or scene transition

Learning Objectives

  • •Explain the different ways to get Medicare benefits
  • •Describe eligibility for Part A and Part B
  • •Understand Part A and Part B coverage
  • •Explain Original Medicare premiums and cost-sharing
  • •Describe Medigap and when Medicare is secondary to employer plans

Key Content

  • Medicare is federal health insurance for people 65+ and those under 65 with certain disabilities or ESRD.
  • Part A: Hospital, SNF (up to 100 days), hospice, home health. Part B: Doctors, outpatient, preventive care.
  • Part A is usually premium-free if you have 40 quarters of work. Part B has a standard premium; higher income = higher premium.
  • Original Medicare = Part A + B (fee-for-service). Part D is always through a private plan.
  • Medigap fills gaps in Original Medicare — copays, coinsurance, deductibles. Best enrollment window: 6 months after Part B.
  • When you have employer coverage, Medicare may be secondary. Special Enrollment Period applies when you lose employer coverage.

Key takeaway

Know the four parts, eligibility, and when Medicare is primary vs secondary.
